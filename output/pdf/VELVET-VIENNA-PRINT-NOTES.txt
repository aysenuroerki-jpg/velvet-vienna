VELVET VIENNA BUSINESS CARD - PRINT NOTES

Finished trim size: 85 x 55 mm
Artwork size: 91 x 61 mm
Bleed: 3 mm on every edge
Resolution: 300 DPI
Color preparation: High-resolution RGB print PDF
Pages: Page 1 front, Page 2 back

Printer setup:
- Print at 100% scale. Do not use Fit to page.
- Trim 3 mm from every edge to reach the finished 85 x 55 mm size.
- Ask the printer to convert colors with its own press/paper CMYK profile; this preserves the intended deep burgundy more reliably than a generic CMYK conversion.
- Recommended paper: 350-400 gsm premium matte or soft-touch laminated stock.
- Rounded corners are optional; use a small 3 mm radius if desired.
- The gold appearance is printed artwork. For real metallic foil, ask the printer to create a separate foil plate from the gold logo and accent areas.
